# Product FAQ

## Accounts

**How do I reset my password?**
Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link. The link expires after 30 minutes. If you no longer have access to your email, contact support with your account ID.

**How do I enable two-factor authentication?**
Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication. We support authenticator apps (TOTP) and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported.

## Billing

**What is the refund policy?**
Full refunds are available within 14 days of purchase for annual plans. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and will remain active until the end of the current billing period. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

**Do you offer a free tier?**
Yes. The free tier includes up to 3 projects and 1 GB of storage. It does not include priority support or API access.

## Support

**What are support hours?**
Priority support is available 24/7 for Enterprise plans. Standard support responds within 1 business day, Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00 UTC.

**How do I report a bug?**
Email support@acme.example with steps to reproduce, your browser or client version, and any relevant screenshots. Critical production issues should also include the incident ID.
