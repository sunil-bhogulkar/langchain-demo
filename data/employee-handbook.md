# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

- Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month (18 days per year).
- Sick leave is separate: 10 days per year, and a doctor's note is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
- Unused paid leave carries over up to 5 days into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited.
- Leave must be requested at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal, except for sick leave.

## Remote Work

- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone.
- Fully remote arrangements require an annual review and a signed remote work agreement.

## Expenses

- Expenses under $75 need no receipt but must be categorized in the expense tool.
- Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $50 per day.
- Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.

## Performance Reviews

- Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December.
- Each review includes a self-assessment, peer feedback, and a manager summary.
- Promotion decisions are made in the December cycle only.
